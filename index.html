                How to play

                • Daily flight: seven prompts, the same for everyone, new at 00:00 UTC. You get 25 seconds a round.
                • A correct answer ends the round. A wrong one costs 3 seconds, and you keep guessing.
                • Rarer is better. The obvious answer scores 10. The answer everyone thinks is clever scores 15. The one hidden One in a Swarm answer scores 100.
                • Spelling is forgiving: capitals, accents, punctuation and plurals don't matter, and common short names count (CULC is Clough).
                • Swarm mode: one 45-second clock for the whole run. It only drains while the answer box is active or has text in it. Correct answers add 8 to 16 seconds. A wrong guess costs 3 seconds, a skip costs 5.

                Rarity tiers are editorial guesses, not measured from real players. Fan-made game. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Georgia Institute of Technology.
