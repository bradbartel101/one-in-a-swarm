    A Georgia Tech word game. Rarer answers fly higher.

    Seven prompts, 25 seconds each. Don't say the obvious thing.

    How to play
    • Daily flight: seven prompts, the same for everyone, new at 00:00 UTC. You get 25 seconds a round.
    • A correct answer ends the round and lifts the swarm. A wrong one costs 3 seconds, and you keep guessing.
    • Rarer is better. The obvious answer scores 10 and barely leaves the lawn. The one hidden One in a Swarm answer scores 100. A perfect day reaches the edge of space.
    • Spelling is forgiving: capitals, accents, punctuation and plurals don't matter, short names count (CULC is Clough), and a near-miss asks you to confirm.
    • Infinite mode: one 45-second clock for the whole run. It only drains while the answer box is active or has text in it. Correct answers add 8 to 16 seconds. A wrong guess costs 3, a skip costs 5.

    Rarity tiers are editorial guesses, not measured from real players.
