# VERIFY.md — answers a human should check

Answers marked `"verify": true` in `data/prompts.json` are listed at the bottom of this file.
The list is generated by `tools/build-prompts.js`, and `tests/data.test.js` fails if it drifts
from the JSON. In the v1.0 release audit every flagged answer was checked against a live source:
the confirmed ones were unflagged, and the ones that could not be confirmed were removed from
the game and listed in `HUMAN-TODO.md` for a Tech student to rule on.

To flag a new answer, put `?` before its tier letter in `tools/prompts.src.txt` and add
`?? reason` at the end of the line, then run `npm run build:data`.

## Altitude facts

The facts that slide past on the climb live in `js/facts.js`. The specific numbers were checked
against sources during the v1.1 rebuild (building heights, Brasstown Bald, the National Weather
Service cloud chart, the Baumgartner and Eustace jumps, the SR-71 record). The ones that are
typical or approximate figures rather than single measurements are flagged below. The game
measures altitude from campus, while mountain heights are quoted above sea level, as they always are.

## Things to check that are not single answers

- **Most unflagged answers were written from memory and not individually checked.** The audit's
  spot checks found one stale unflagged answer (a retired major), so expect a few more. A Tech
  student or alum skimming `tools/prompts.src.txt` is the real test.
- **Rarity tiers are my guesses**, not measurements. Which answers are "Common" versus "Deep Cut"
  is an editorial call until real players supply data.
- **Course titles** shown after a correct course-number answer are from memory, and Tech renumbers
  courses. The numbers themselves are the answers; the titles are decoration.
- **"Past or present" prompts** (dining, pro teams, Greek chapters, residence halls) include places
  and chapters that have closed, moved, been renamed, or been suspended. That is intended, but a
  current student may not recognise some of them.
- **`bowls` and `dining` sit at exactly 25 answers.** Removing one answer from either drops it
  below the floor, and the build will refuse it.
- **Rosters keep moving.** NBA, NFL, MLB and coaching lists stop at what I knew when writing; recent
  call-ups and hires are missing, so a correct recent answer may be marked wrong.

## Flagged answers

### Altitude facts (`js/facts.js`, 16 facts)

- [ ] **Skydivers usually step out around 13,000 ft.** A typical figure; drop zones quote anything from 10,000 to 14,000 ft.
- [ ] **Around 36,000 ft the stratosphere begins. The weather is all below you now.** This is the standard-atmosphere figure; the real boundary moves with latitude and season.
- [ ] **Weather balloons burst at around 100,000 ft. The sky overhead is nearly black.** Burst height varies by balloon; "nearly black" is a description, not a measurement.
